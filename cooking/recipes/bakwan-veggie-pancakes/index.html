Bakwan (Indonesian Veggie Pancakes)

Crisp little Indonesian vegetable fritters bound with potato starch and egg — baby-friendly from 1 year old

SERVINGS: 2
PREP: 20 MIN
COOK: 15 MIN
easy
CUISINE: Indonesian
indonesianasianvegetablesfritterspancakesbabybaby-friendlytoddlerquicksavory

Ingredients

For 2 portions:

  • 1 carrot
  • 1 potato
  • 100g cabbage
  • 100g cauliflower
  • 1 spring onion
  • Half a corn cob (optional)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp potato starch
  • ½ tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (low-sodium, or normal)
  • A pinch of salt (skip it if you use normal soy sauce)
  • Oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Slice the potato and the cauliflower, then bring to a boil until almost soft.
  2. Mash the potato and cauliflower slightly — small chunks are fine. Put them in a prep bowl.
  3. Finely slice the carrot, cabbage and spring onion, then add them to the prep bowl. If you use corn, slice the kernels straight off the cob.
  4. Add the 2 tbsp of potato starch and the ½ tbsp of flour to the vegetable mix.
  5. Add the 1 tbsp of soy sauce. With low-sodium soy sauce, add a pinch of salt for taste; with normal soy sauce you can leave the salt out.
  6. Beat the egg and add it to the bowl. Mix everything until the batter is smooth.
  7. Heat a pan, add a bit of oil, and drop the batter in small portions like little pancakes.
  8. Fry until golden brown on both sides.

Notes

For babies

This recipe has been tested and can be given to babies over 1 year old. For younger babies over 6 months, use low-sodium soy sauce, reduce the amount, and leave the salt out.

Tips

  • The potato starch is what makes the fritters crisp on the outside and keeps them together — do not swap it all out for flour.
  • Slice the raw vegetables as finely as you can so they cook through in the pan.
  • Fry over medium heat, around 170°C: too hot and the outside browns before the carrot and cabbage are tender.
