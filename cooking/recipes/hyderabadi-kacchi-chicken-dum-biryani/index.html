Hyderabadi Kacchi Chicken Dum Biryani

Raw-marinated bone-in chicken layered under parboiled basmati and steamed sealed — built on ready-made bawang goreng instead of frying your own onions

SERVINGS: 4
PREP: 45 MIN
COOK: 55 MIN
medium
CUISINE: Indian
indianasianchickenricebiryanione-potdumbasmatispicybawang-goreng

Ingredients

Serves 4

For the chicken marinade

  • 900 g bone-in chicken, cut into large pieces
  • 250 g plain full-fat yogurt, whisked smooth
  • 80 g bawang goreng, crushed in your hand
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 4 green chillies, slit
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 30 ml neutral oil or ghee

For the rice

  • 500 g basmati rice, aged if possible
  • 3 l water
  • 3 tbsp salt
  • 5 green cardamom pods
  • 5 cloves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp shahi jeera (caraway seeds)

For layering

  • 40 g bawang goreng
  • 25 g fresh mint leaves
  • 20 g fresh coriander, chopped
  • 60 ml ghee
  • 60 ml warm milk + 1 pinch turmeric
  • 1 tsp kewra water or rose water (optional)

Instructions

  1. Marinate (4 hours minimum, overnight best). Mix the chicken with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chilli powder, garam masala, turmeric, salt, green chillies, lemon juice, oil, half the mint, half the coriander, and 80 g of the bawang goreng crushed in your hand. Refrigerate.
  2. Soak the rice (30 min). Rinse until the water runs clear, then soak in cold water. Drain.
  3. Parboil the rice (4–5 min). Bring the water to a rolling boil with the salt and all the whole spices. Add the rice and boil 4–5 minutes only — a grain should still snap in the centre. Drain at once and spread out.
  4. Layer. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Spread it with all its marinade across the base of a heavy pot. Pile the drained rice on top. Scatter over the remaining 40 g bawang goreng, mint and coriander.
  5. Finish the top. Stir the turmeric into the warm milk and drizzle over the rice in streaks, not all over. Add the kewra water and pour the ghee across the surface.
  6. Seal and start high (5 min). Seal the pot with foil, then press the lid down hard on top. Cook on high until you hear it hissing and steam is clearly trapped.
  7. Dum on low (35 min). Drop to the lowest possible heat, on a tawa or heat diffuser if you have one. Do not open it at any point.
  8. Rest (15 min). Turn off the heat, leave sealed. Then open, dig down to a thick piece near the bottom and check it is cooked through; if not, reseal and give it 10 more minutes on low. Lift gently from one side so the white and brown grains stay separate.

Notes

Tips and tricks

  • Bawang goreng does the work of home-fried birista here: 120 g of the packaged stuff stands in for about 4 large onions, and it is already deep golden and dry. Buy the plain kind with nothing but shallot or onion, oil and salt.
  • Because there is no frying oil to reserve, the marinade gets 30 ml of neutral oil or ghee instead. Do not leave it out — it carries the spices and keeps the yogurt from tightening.
  • Crush the marinade portion of the bawang goreng in your hand so it dissolves into the yogurt. That is what gives Hyderabadi biryani its colour without saffron.
  • Keep the layering portion uncrushed and add it at the last moment, so it stays in recognisable pieces through the steam.
  • Undercook the rice on purpose. 4–5 minutes is enough; it finishes in the steam. Rice boiled soft turns to mush.
  • Salt the rice water heavily — it should taste like seawater. That is the only chance to season the rice itself.
  • Shahi jeera, not cumin. Caraway is the signature aroma. Worth buying.
  • Kewra water: 1 tsp is plenty. It turns soapy fast.
  • Without saffron, apply the turmeric milk in streaks only, or skip it. Spread evenly it just makes everything uniformly yellow; the contrast between white and onion-browned grains looks right on its own.
  • Use a heavy pot with a tight lid. Thin pots scorch the bottom layer.
  • Do not peek during the dum. Every lift of the lid loses the steam doing the cooking.
  • Kacchi means the meat goes in raw, so use bone-in pieces. Boneless breast overcooks before the rice is done.

Variations

  • Safer first attempt: brown the marinated chicken for 10 minutes in a pan before layering. Not strictly kacchi, but nothing will be undercooked.
  • Vegetable version: 700 g mixed cauliflower, potato and carrot instead of chicken; same marinade, reduce the dum to 25 minutes.

Serving

Serve with mirchi ka salan and a thin yogurt chutney with onion and cucumber.
