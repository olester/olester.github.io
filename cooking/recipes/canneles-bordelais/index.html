Cannelés Bordelais

Traditional French pastries with a caramelized crust and soft custard center - cassonade recipe (half batch)

SERVINGS: 12
PREP: 30 MIN
COOK: 75 MIN
hard
CUISINE: French
dessertfrenchpastrybordeauxtraditional

Ingredients

For approximately 12 cannelés:

  • 500g semi-skimmed organic milk
  • 130g organic brown sugar (cassonade) - first portion
  • 32g organic butter
  • 1 Madagascar vanilla bean
  • 150g T80 organic flour (or whole wheat/all-purpose flour)
  • 130g organic brown sugar (cassonade) - second portion
  • 100g organic whole eggs (approximately 2 large eggs)
  • 75g Saint James rum
  • “Slider” release agent for greasing molds

Instructions

STEP 1: Infuse the milk

Put the first portion of sugar (130g) in a pan, then add the milk, butter, and split vanilla bean. Heat until just simmering and sugar is dissolved. Remove from heat and let cool to exactly 63°C (145°F) before proceeding - this temperature is important for the proper texture.

TIP: Use a kitchen thermometer to check the milk temperature at 63°C for best results!

STEP 2: Mix the batter

  1. Combine the second portion of sugar (130g) with flour
  2. Whisk in the eggs until smooth
  3. Gradually incorporate the milk mixture (at 63°C) and rum
  4. Strain through a fine sieve to ensure a smooth batter

STEP 3: Rest (Essential Step!)

Refrigerate the batter for 24-48 hours. This resting period is crucial for developing the proper texture and flavor.

STEP 4: Prepare molds

  1. Grease cannelé molds thoroughly with the release agent
  2. Chill the greased molds in the refrigerator

STEP 5: Bake

  1. Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F)
  2. Fill molds ¾ full with the batter
  3. Bake for 15 minutes at high heat (230°C)
  4. Reduce temperature to 180°C (350°F)
  5. Continue baking for 45-60 minutes until deeply caramelized
  6. The cannelés should have a dark, caramelized exterior

STEP 6: Unmold and cool

Unmold immediately while hot and cool on a rack. This prevents them from becoming soggy.

Notes

For perfect cannelés:

  1. Temperature precision - The 63°C milk temperature is crucial for texture
  2. Resting time - Don’t skip the 24-48 hour rest period
  3. High initial heat - The 230°C start creates the signature crust
  4. Deep caramelization - Don’t be afraid of the dark color; it’s characteristic
  5. Immediate unmolding - Prevents sogginess

Keys to success:

  • Use proper cannelé molds (copper is traditional, silicone works)
  • The batter should be very liquid before resting
  • The two-stage baking (high then low) is essential for the texture contrast
  • Cannelés are best eaten the day they’re baked

Texture characteristics:

  • Exterior: Dark, caramelized, crispy crust
  • Interior: Soft, custardy, slightly chewy center
  • The contrast between crust and center is what makes cannelés special

Storage:

  • Best consumed within 24 hours of baking
  • Store at room temperature in an airtight container
  • Can be briefly reheated in a hot oven (200°C for 3-5 minutes) to restore crispness

About the ingredients:

  • Cassonade (brown sugar): Gives a deeper flavor and color
  • T80 flour: Traditional choice, but all-purpose flour works well
  • Rum: Saint James or another good quality dark rum is traditional
  • Vanilla bean: Madagascar vanilla is preferred for its rich flavor

The secret: Patience with the resting time and precision with temperatures will give you authentic Bordeaux cannelés!
