Browned Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Thick, bakery-style cookies made with nutty browned butter and an overnight chill

SERVINGS: 13
PREP: 30 MIN
COOK: 12 MIN
medium
dessertcookieschocolatebakingbrowned-butter

Ingredients

For approximately 13 cookies of 80g each:

  • 210g browned butter (from approximately one 250g block of unsalted butter)
  • 300g plain flour
  • 1 tbsp (8g) cornflour
  • ¾ tsp (4g) bicarbonate of soda
  • 1 tsp (4g) baking powder
  • 1 tsp (6g) fine sea salt
  • 140g light brown soft sugar
  • 90g white granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 tsp vanilla bean paste
  • 250-300g chocolate chips or chopped chocolate

Instructions

STEP 1: Brown the butter

  1. Brown a 250g block of butter until the milk solids are a deep golden brown with a nutty aroma
  2. Cool until it is opaque and soft, like room-temperature butter
  3. Weigh out 210g of the browned butter for the dough

STEP 2: Cream

Cream the 210g browned butter with both sugars for 2-3 minutes, until lightened.

STEP 3: Wet ingredients

Beat in the egg, egg yolk and vanilla bean paste.

STEP 4: Dry ingredients

  1. Mix together the flour, cornflour, bicarbonate of soda, baking powder and salt
  2. Fold into the wet ingredients until just combined

STEP 5: Chocolate

Fold through the chocolate.

STEP 6: Portion and chill

  1. Portion into 80g balls, shaping them slightly taller than they are wide
  2. Chill overnight (or at least 12 hours)

STEP 7: Bake

  1. Bake straight from the fridge at 200°C, no fan, for 10-12 minutes
  2. They are ready when the edges are golden and the centres are still a little soft

STEP 8: Rest

  1. Bang the tray on the work top a couple of times straight out of the oven
  2. Leave the cookies on the baking tray for 10 minutes before moving them

Notes

Keys to success:

  1. Cool the butter properly - browned butter used warm melts the sugars and spreads the cookies flat
  2. Weigh the butter after browning - browning drives off water, so 250g of butter yields roughly 210g
  3. Tall dough balls - shaping them taller than wide gives thick cookies with ridged tops
  4. The overnight chill - hydrates the flour, deepens the flavour and stops the dough spreading
  5. Bake from cold - straight from the fridge, no resting on the counter first

Mistakes to avoid:

  • Overmixing after the flour goes in → tough cookies
  • Baking warm dough → thin, greasy cookies
  • Overbaking → the centres should still look soft when they come out; they set on the tray

Storage:

  • Baked cookies keep 3 days in an airtight container at room temperature
  • Portioned dough balls freeze well for up to 3 months - bake from frozen, adding 1-2 minutes
